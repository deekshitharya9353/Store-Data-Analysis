# 📊 Store Sales Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Store Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze store sales performance across different dimensions such as orders, revenue, products, payment methods, promotions, shipping costs, discounts, regions, store locations, and customer types.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPIs, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall store sales performance
- Track total orders and total sales amount
- Compare monthly order volume and revenue
- Identify high-performing products
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Analyze revenue generated through promotions
- Compare shipping costs across products
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts, orders, and revenue
- Allow users to filter the analysis by region, store, and customer type

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard provides three key performance indicators:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Order** | Total number of orders |
| **Total Amount** | Overall sales/revenue generated |
| **Average Duration** | Average delivery/order duration |

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the store's overall performance.

---

### 🔹 Monthly Order vs Amount

A combination chart compares:

- Monthly order count
- Monthly sales amount

This helps identify monthly sales trends and periods with higher or lower order activity.

---

### 🔹 Product: Order vs Amount

The product analysis compares sales performance across different products such as:

- Chair
- Desk
- Laptop
- Monitor
- Phone
- Printer
- Tablet

This visualization helps identify products contributing significantly to overall sales.

---

### 🔹 Mode of Payment

A pie chart shows the distribution of different payment methods:

- Cash
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Gift Card
- Online

This helps understand customer payment preferences.

---

### 🔹 Revenue Based on Promotion

This analysis compares revenue generated through different promotional strategies:

- FREESHIP
- WINTER15
- No Promotion
- SAVE10

It helps evaluate the effectiveness of promotional campaigns.

---

### 🔹 Shipping Cost Based on Products

This chart compares shipping costs across different product categories.

It can help businesses identify products with relatively higher shipping expenses and support logistics cost optimization.

---

### 🔹 Discount: Order vs Amount

This visualization analyzes the relationship between discount levels and:

- Number of orders
- Sales amount

It helps understand how different discount percentages affect customer purchasing behavior and revenue.

---

- Store B

These filters allow users to perform more focused analysis and compare different business segments.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- The dashboard tracks **1,500 total orders**.
- Total sales amount is approximately **₹43.80 Lakhs**.
- Average order/delivery duration is approximately **6.04 days**.
- Monthly sales and order volumes fluctuate throughout the year.
- Laptop, Printer, and Tablet show relatively strong sales amounts compared with other products.
- Cash and Online payments represent significant portions of the payment mix.
- Promotional campaigns generate different levels of revenue, allowing their performance to be compared.
- Higher discount levels can be analyzed against order volume and revenue to understand discount effectiveness.
- Shipping costs vary across products, providing opportunities for logistics optimization.

> **Note:** Insights change dynamically when different Region, Store Location, or Customer Type filters are selected.
